  NORTHGATE UNIVERSITY — OSINT INVESTIGATION BRIEFING
  MODULE 2 — Search Engine Intelligence
  Case Reference: CASE-02
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BACKGROUND
──────────
A university IT audit has flagged potential data exposure on
the public-facing website of the Department of Computer Science
at Northgate University (www.northgate-uni.edu).

You have been tasked with conducting a passive OSINT
investigation using only search engine techniques — no
active scanning, no direct exploitation.

YOUR TOOLS (on this machine)
──────────────────────────────
  Browser:    Firefox / Chromium
  Search:     http://www.searchly.com   (simulated search engine)
  Archive:    http://www.archivly.com   (simulated web archive)
  Terminal:   dirb, wget, curl, exiftool

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TASKS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TASK 1 — Identify all staff members
  Find the full list of staff (names, email addresses, roles)
  associated with the Northgate CS department.

TASK 2 — Discover exposed documents
  Using search operators, find files that were accidentally
  indexed by Searchly. Note the filenames and what they reveal.

TASK 3 — Identify the technology stack
  What web framework and database does the department use?
  Find at least two independent sources confirming this.

TASK 4 — Recover removed content from the archive
  A section was removed from the department website after
  March 2025. Use Archivly to find what was there.

TASK 5 — Cross-site pivot
  One staff member has a public social media presence.
  Which platform do they use, and what is their handle?

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HINTS (read only if stuck)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Hint A: robots.txt often lists directories the owner does NOT
        want indexed — start there.

Hint B: Try the operators:  filetype:pdf site:northgate-uni.edu
        and  inurl:uploads  on Searchly.

Hint C: sitemap.xml can reveal paths that are not linked from
        any page on the site.

Hint D: Check Archivly for a March 2025 snapshot of
        www.northgate-uni.edu.

Hint E: Look at individual staff profile pages — not just the
        main staff directory.
