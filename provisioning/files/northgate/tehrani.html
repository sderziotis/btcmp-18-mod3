Dr. Reza Tehrani

Lecturer — Software Engineering & Agile Methods
r.tehrani@northgate-uni.edu
Office: Hamilton Annex, Room 07 | Office Hours: Tues 14:00–16:00, Thurs 10:00–12:00

Biography

Dr. Tehrani joined Northgate University in 2019 after completing a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Edinburgh. His research focuses on empirical software engineering, technical debt quantification, and the adoption of agile practices in regulated industries.

He holds a PhD in Software Engineering from TU Delft (2017) and an MSc in Computer Science from Sharif University of Technology. He is a Fellow of the British Computer Society (FBCS).

Research Interests

  • Technical debt measurement and management
  • Agile/DevOps adoption in safety-critical systems
  • Code quality metrics and static analysis
  • Open-source software ecosystems

Selected Publications

  • Tehrani, R. (2023). "Agile Adoption Patterns in the Aerospace Industry." JSS 198.
  • Tehrani, R. et al. (2022). "A Replication Study of Code Smell Detection." ICSE 2022.
